如何购买 PU / How to Buy PU

🇨🇳 中文教程

  1. 安装 MetaMask（或任意 EVM 钱包）并添加 Polygon 网络（Chain ID: 137）
  2. 确保钱包内有少量 POL（用于支付 Gas，一般不超过 $0.01）
  3. 将 USDC（Polygon 版本）充入钱包
  4. 点击上方按钮打开 QuickSwap，或手动设置：
    From: USDC
    To: 0xB75A2C4f8517e9b40E8C659731968C55955580d5
  5. 建议 滑点：少量 ≤1%；大额 3%–5%
  6. 确认后等待链上确认（通常 <5 秒）

🇺🇸 English Guide

  1. Install MetaMask (or any EVM wallet) and add the Polygon network (Chain ID: 137)
  2. Have a small amount of POL for gas (usually < $0.01 per swap)
  3. Bridge or deposit USDC (Polygon) to your wallet
  4. Click the button above to open QuickSwap, or manually set:
    From: USDC
    To: 0xB75A2C4f8517e9b40E8C659731968C55955580d5
  5. Recommended slippage: ≤1% for small trades; 3–5% for larger ones
  6. Confirm and wait for on-chain confirmation (usually <5 seconds)
